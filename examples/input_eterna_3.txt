Name: Margaret L.
Age: 58
How did you hear about Eterna?: My daughter installed it on my phone for my birthday.
How long have you been using Eterna?: About 3 weeks (started using less around week 2).

What were you hoping to get from Eterna?
Honestly I'm not sure. My daughter said it would help me remember things from my teaching days — I taught fourth grade for 31 years and she has been after me to write a book about it. I thought maybe this would help. But I'm not a person who naturally journals. I open the app, I see the microphone button, and I just stare at it. I don't know what I'm supposed to say.

What worked for you?
When I did manage to record something, the transcription was accurate. The first week I told a story about a student named Jamie I had in 1994 and I was surprised how well the app captured the names and the year. That part felt a little magic.

What didn't work?
Two things, really. First, the empty mic. I needed prompts. Something like "what's a memory from work that came back to you this week?" — or even just a question of the day. Anything to start me off. I'm sure younger people know what to talk about, but I didn't.

The second thing is harder to put into words. I used the app for almost three weeks. I told it about my husband, my three grandkids, my old classroom, three of my favorite students. I expected, by week three, that opening the app would feel like opening a book that knew me. Instead it still feels like talking to a recording device that has never met me. It doesn't ask me follow-ups. It doesn't say "you mentioned Mr. Patterson last week, want to add to that?" The app on day 22 felt exactly like the app on day one — just with more files inside it.

I stopped using it around week 2. I will probably try again if my daughter pushes me, but I don't think it's for me as it is now.

Would you recommend Eterna?
Not yet. Maybe to my younger nephew. Not to anyone over 50.

Anything else?
Thank you for asking. The young man who made this clearly cares. Tell him I said that.

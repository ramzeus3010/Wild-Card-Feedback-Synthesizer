What is your overall impression of Eterna?
Absolutely stellar. I’ve tried every journaling app on the market (Day One, Reflection, etc.) and I always bounce off because typing out my thoughts after staring at spreadsheets for nine hours feels like more work. Eterna has completely solved that. I just talk for five minutes during my commute home, and the AI summaries are incredibly accurate at capturing the core themes of my day. It's lowered the friction to zero.

What features or improvements would you like to see? (Optional)
Honestly, the app is practically flawless for my use case. If I had to throw out one completely non-essential "nice-to-have" idea, it would be the ability to change the default haptic feedback vibration pattern when you press down the record button. Right now it’s a solid, heavy thud—which feels premium!—but it would be kind of neat if there were a couple of different subtle click options, like a double-tap rhythm or a lighter tick. Again, this is a 1% cosmetic thing that has zero impact on how great the app is, just a bit of extra polish for tactile nerds. Keep doing what you're doing!

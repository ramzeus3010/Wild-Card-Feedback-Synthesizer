Ramazan,

Promised you a writeup, here it is. Bug report from a friendly nerd, not a roast.

Setup: iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 18.4, fresh install May 1. ~40 voice memos over 14 days, deliberately stress-testing across noisy bars, the gym, in the car. I also wrote a tiny script to re-import the same audio files multiple times to see how stable the model output is. That's where the headline finding is, so I'm putting it first.

THE WEIRD THING
I re-imported the same .m4a three times across three days. Same audio. Identical bytes. I got back three meaningfully different memory summaries — different tags, different inferred dates ("last Tuesday" vs "May 8" vs no date at all), and one summary attributed a quote to a person whose name was never spoken in the audio. The user-facing memory cards were visibly different from each other. This isn't a rounding-error difference, this is "is the same input producing the same output." For an app whose whole promise is reliable memory, that broke trust for me. I won't recommend to friends until I can play back a memory and trust what it says about who, what, and when. Happy to send you the audio files and the raw JSON if useful.

OTHER BUGS (smaller)
- Date search sometimes returns memories that don't actually contain that date — looks like fuzzy-matching to adjacent dates
- People-tagging once attributed a quote to me when I was clearly quoting someone else (same family of problem as above)
- First recording after a phone restart sometimes loses the first 1-2 seconds of audio

WHAT'S GOOD
- Capture flow is genuinely better than iOS Voice Memos
- The people graph is the best part of the app
- Solid performance, no crashes in 14 days

THEORY (take or leave)
If you're hitting an LLM at write-time without pinning seed/temperature, and not caching the structured output keyed on the audio hash, this is exactly what you'd see. If you're already on it, ignore me.

Will I pay? Curious enough to keep playing for free. Won't pay or recommend until same-input-same-output is fixed. That's the thing.
